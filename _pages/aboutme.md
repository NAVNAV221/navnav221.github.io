I'm **Nave Ben Naim** — a security researcher who writes here as **navnav221**.
This site, NavSec Blog, is where I publish what I learn: vulnerability research,
CTF and Hack The Box writeups, protocol internals, and lately a fair amount
about how context shapes what AI models can actually do.

### What I work on

- **Vulnerability research.** My first CVE was
  [CVE-2022-34389](/2023-04-28-dell-cve_2022_34389/) in Dell's remote assistance
  flow — a rate-limiting flaw that let an attacker impersonate a legitimate Dell
  customer to a support technician.
- **CTFs and Hack The Box.** I play CTFs regularly, including BSides TLV, and
  write up the machines and challenges I find interesting. Not walkthroughs —
  the conclusions.
- **Active Directory and Kerberos internals.** Understanding the protocol beats
  memorizing the tool. That's the whole ethos here: *don't be a script kiddie*.
- **AI and context engineering.** At Naboo I work on giving models the right
  organizational context — because a smart model doesn't make up for bad context.
