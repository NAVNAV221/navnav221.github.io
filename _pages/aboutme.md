I'm **Nave Ben Naim** - a Founding Engineer at [Naboo.ai](https://naboo.ai),
writing here as **navnav221**. My background is in AI engineering and
cybersecurity. This site, NavSec Blog, is where I publish what I learn.

### What I work on

- **AI and context engineering.** At Naboo I work on context-aware AI -
  giving models the right organizational context, because
  [a smart model doesn't make up for bad context](/2026-06-21-smart-model-bad-context/).
- **Vulnerability research.** My first CVE was
  [CVE-2022-34389](/2023-04-28-dell-cve_2022_34389/) in Dell's remote assistance
  flow - a rate-limiting flaw that let an attacker impersonate a legitimate Dell
  customer to a support technician.
- **CTFs and Hack The Box.** I play CTFs regularly, including BSides TLV, and
  [write up](/htb/) the machines and challenges I find interesting. Not
  walkthroughs - the conclusions.
- **Active Directory and Kerberos internals.** Understanding the protocol beats
  memorizing the tool. That's the ethos here: *don't be a script kiddie*.
